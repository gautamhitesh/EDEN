EDEN - Emergency Drive for Everything Needed
==============================================
EDEN drives on this stick. Labels follow the pattern EDEN-<number>-<ROLE>:
  EDEN-1-BOOT    bootable operating systems (.iso)
  EDEN-2-KIT     offline AI, encyclopedias, encrypted vault  (start here: README.txt)
  EDEN-3-SPARE   free space for backups (optional)
  (VTOYEFI is the boot loader - never open, change or rename it)

THIS DRIVE: EDEN-1-BOOT

Put here:     .iso files only. The boot menu lists every .iso on this drive.
Do NOT:       rename, unzip or edit the .iso files, or store anything else here.
              Personal files here are NOT encrypted - use the vault on EDEN-2-KIT.
